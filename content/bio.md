I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai. Before joining BNUZ, I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Texas A&M University in 2022 and served as a Postdoctoral Research Associate within the same department for two years.

My research focuses on foundational statistical learning and applied data science. My research interests include multiple testing, watermarking in large language models, Markov chain Monte Carlo methodology, Bayesian variable selection, and statistical methods for single-cell RNA sequencing and microbiome data.

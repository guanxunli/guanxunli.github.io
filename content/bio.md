I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at Beijing Normal University at Zhuhai. Before joining BNU Zhuhai, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University.

My research interests span multiple testing, watermarking in large language models, Markov chain Monte Carlo methodology, Bayesian variable selection, and statistical methods for single-cell RNA sequencing and microbiome data.
